# OEM & Aftermarket Solutions

Starklips Otomotiv provides automotive clips, fasteners and assembly products for OEM and aftermarket supply channels.

## OEM Supply

We support automotive manufacturers and suppliers with fastening and assembly solutions for vehicle production and component manufacturing.

Our supply capabilities include:

- Automotive clips
- Plastic fasteners
- Metal fasteners
- Retainers
- Trim clips
- Panel clips
- Assembly components
- Automotive connection parts

## Aftermarket Supply

Starklips Otomotiv also supplies products for the automotive aftermarket through distributors, wholesalers, importers and spare parts companies.

We work with:

- Automotive aftermarket distributors
- Spare parts wholesalers
- Importers
- Automotive parts retailers
- Industrial suppliers
- Service and repair product distributors
